# Privacy Policy for Auto Scroll for YouTube & TikTok

**Last updated:** November 8, 2026

Auto Scroll for YouTube & TikTok ("the Extension", "we", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
- **Zero Data Collection**: We do **not** collect, store, track, or sell any personal information, browsing history, user identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- **Local Settings Only**: Any configuration changes you make (e.g., scroll delay time, skip ads/live toggles, language preferences) are stored strictly locally on your device via Chrome's `storage.local` API. This data never leaves your browser.

## 2. Permissions Justification
- `storage`: Required exclusively to remember your personalized settings across browser sessions on your local machine.
- Host permissions (`*://*.youtube.com/*`, `*://*.tiktok.com/*`): Required exclusively to run content scripts on YouTube and TikTok to detect when a video ends and automate the scroll gesture. No data from these websites is ever captured, logged, or transmitted externally.

## 3. Remote Code & External Services
- The Extension contains **zero remote code**. All script files, styles, and locales are packaged entirely within the published extension bundle.
- There are no external analytics, trackers, or advertising SDKs embedded.

## 4. Third-Party Links
The Extension includes an optional voluntary support link to Ko-fi (`https://ko-fi.com/poordev1997`). Clicking this link opens the third-party website in a new tab, subject to Ko-fi's own privacy policy and terms.
